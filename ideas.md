## Idea 1: "Florida Naturalist" — Organic Warmth

**Design Movement:** Biophilic Design meets Southern Craftsman

**Core Principles:**
- Earthy, warm tones drawn from Florida's wetlands and pine forests
- Asymmetric, editorial-style layouts with generous whitespace
- Hand-crafted texture through subtle grain overlays and organic shapes
- Photography-forward storytelling with full-bleed imagery

**Color Philosophy:**
Deep forest green (#1B3A2D) as the anchor, paired with warm sand (#E8D9B5), clay terracotta (#C4622D), and off-white cream (#FAF7F2). Evokes the natural Florida landscape — pine straw, Spanish moss, and Boggy Creek.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Staggered editorial grid — alternating left/right image-text blocks, oversized section numbers as decorative elements, full-bleed hero with diagonal clip-path transition.

**Signature Elements:**
- Leaf/blade-of-grass SVG motifs as section dividers
- Circular image crops for team/testimonial portraits
- Textured linen-like background on alternating sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Slow, deliberate scroll animations — content fades in from below as you scroll, evoking the pace of nature.

**Animation:**
Fade-up on scroll for cards, parallax on hero image, subtle hover lift on service cards.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — authoritative, Southern elegance
- Body: Source Sans 3 — clean, readable
- Accent: Playfair Display Italic for pull quotes

## Idea 2: "Precision Green" — Modern Professional

**Design Movement:** Swiss Grid meets Contemporary Service Brand

**Core Principles:**
- High-contrast, bold typography with strict grid discipline
- Minimal color palette — one dominant green, black, white
- Data-forward: pricing tables, service checklists, clear CTAs
- Trust signals front and center (licensed, insured, reviews)

**Color Philosophy:**
Vivid lawn green (#2D7A3A) as the hero accent, near-black charcoal (#1A1A1A) for text, clean white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds. Small pops of golden yellow (#F5C842) for CTAs. Communicates precision, professionalism, reliability.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Strict 12-column grid, full-width hero with bold headline overlay, card-based service grid, sticky top navigation bar.

**Signature Elements:**
- Bold oversized numbers for stats (e.g., "500+ Lawns Served")
- Checklist-style service breakdowns
- Green gradient stripe as a recurring horizontal divider

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Crisp, fast micro-interactions — instant hover color fills, sharp button press states.

**Animation:**
Counter animations for stats, slide-in from left for service cards, sharp fade on navigation.

**Typography System:**
- Display: DM Sans Bold — geometric, modern
- Body: DM Sans Regular
- Accent: DM Mono for pricing/numbers

## Idea 3: "Tributary Roots" — Community-Centric Warmth ✅ SELECTED

**Design Movement:** New American Vernacular — warm, community-rooted, approachable luxury

**Core Principles:**
- Deep connection to the Tributary community identity (nature trails, lakefront, outdoor living)
- Warm, inviting color palette that feels like a Florida evening
- Asymmetric hero layout with bold serif headline and lush photography
- Generous whitespace with purposeful accent color moments

**Color Philosophy:**
Rich moss green (oklch(0.35 0.12 145)) as the primary brand color, warm cream (oklch(0.97 0.02 90)) for backgrounds, golden amber (oklch(0.75 0.15 75)) for CTAs and highlights, deep charcoal (oklch(0.18 0.01 60)) for text. Evokes the lush Florida landscape and the warmth of a community you belong to.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Offset hero with text left and image right (clipped at an angle), services in a 3-column asymmetric grid, testimonials in a horizontal scroll strip, booking section as a full-width warm-toned panel.

**Signature Elements:**
- Angled/diagonal section transitions (clip-path) between major sections
- Thin botanical line-art SVG accents (grass blade, leaf)
- Amber dot/badge accents on service cards

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Warm, welcoming — hover states use gentle color fills and slight scale transforms. Booking calendar feels approachable, not clinical.

**Animation:**
Scroll-triggered fade-up with slight Y translation, staggered card entrance, smooth calendar date selection transitions.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Lora (serif) — warm, literary, community-feel
- Body: Nunito Sans — friendly, readable, modern
- Accent: Lora Italic for taglines and pull quotes
